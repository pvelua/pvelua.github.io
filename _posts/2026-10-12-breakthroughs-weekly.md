### [A proof that 4-to-1 games are NP-hard advances Khot's conjectures](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/179/)

**ECCC** · 14 Sep 2026 · *Complexity theory*

Yumou Fei, Dor Minzer and Shuo Wang show that distinguishing perfectly satisfiable 4-to-1 games from nearly unsatisfiable ones is NP-hard, which confirms a weakened form of Khot's 2-to-1 games conjecture. As consequences, no algorithm can efficiently colour a 3-colourable graph with any fixed number of colours unless P equals NP, and a related hardness result follows for hypergraphs. The report is not yet peer reviewed and was first posted in a rough state; a fuller revision appeared on 8 October. Quanta Magazine's coverage this month quotes Ryan O'Donnell of Carnegie Mellon calling it "another truly great one", which is how it reached wider attention.

### [Francis Halzen wins the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for IceCube neutrino work](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-10-06-francis-halzen-wins-2026-nobel-prize-in-physics)

**Daily Maverick** · 6 Oct 2026 · *Physics prize*

The Nobel committee announced the 2026 physics prize on 6 October, citing Halzen's contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. This is an award, not a new result: it recognises earlier experimental work that opened a way to observe cosmic sources through neutrinos rather than light. The Nobel Foundation's own page could not be retrieved this run, so this item cites secondary coverage.
