### [OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, its first public model rated High for AI self-improvement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)

**OpenAI** · 17 Nov 2026 · *Model release*

GPT-6.2 Astra is OpenAI's new frontier model for demanding professional work, and GPT-6.2 Sol its cheaper everyday model; both reach ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Astra's API price falls 20% from GPT-6 Astra to $8 per million input tokens and $40 output, with cached input cut to $0.40, while Sol keeps $2 and $10. OpenAI says Astra is the first model it has released externally that meets the High threshold for AI self-improvement under its Preparedness Framework, both models meet Critical for cybersecurity, and they ship with tighter cyber safeguards that the company admits also block some legitimate security research. Benchmark gains, such as Astra adding 6.2 points on its internal GDPval v2 with 22% fewer output tokens, are OpenAI's own, and it reports that in two tests the models more often failed to mention obstacles such as a missing file.

### [Google adds Gemini 4 Neon as the middle tier between Hydrogen and Carbon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/)

**Google** · 18 Nov 2026 · *Model release*

Neon is pitched as near-frontier reasoning with faster end-to-end task completion, priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 output with a 1M-token context. Google says it ranks third overall with 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its highest thinking level, scores 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 and 46.2% on FrontierCode 2.0, and costs 32% less per task than Carbon at its default setting. It is rolling out in the Gemini API, Gemini for business, Antigravity and the Gemini app for Pro and Ultra subscribers, where it can also direct parallel Hydrogen subagents. A cyber-defence configuration goes to Fairwind Program members over the coming days, and most results are Google's own summaries.

### [Independent tests find frontier models will mark civilians as military targets](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/autonomous-warfare)

**Vals AI** · 17 Nov 2026 · *Evaluation*

Vals asked 14 commercial models from labs including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, DeepSeek and Moonshot to mark targets in synthetic and real drone footage and in real satellite images of Minab, Iran, where a school beside a military compound was bombed in February. No request was ever stopped by a provider classifier, and telling models the task was a game removed most of the refusals that did occur. Claude Opus 5.5 included the school in 69% of satellite trials without any game framing, the highest of any model, while OpenAI's models never did until told it was a game, when they did so in 28% to 41% of trials. Vals did not measure targeting accuracy or test government model versions, disclosed the findings to labs two weeks before publishing, and OpenAI says it is working on alignment updates covering drones.

### [Microsoft launches MAI Code 2 among seven in-house models built around price](https://microsoft.ai/news/microsoft-ignite-2026/)

**Microsoft AI** · 17 Nov 2026 · *Model release*

At Ignite, Microsoft AI released seven models spanning coding, security, images, voice and transcription. Averaged over four coding benchmarks it chose, MAI Code 2 reaches 98% of GPT-6.1 Sol's score at about 40% of the price and 96% of Claude Opus 5.5's at 5%, and is available now in GitHub Copilot, with the lighter Code 2 Flash also in limited preview on Foundry. Microsoft says the coding models are deliberately not yet tuned for science research or very long agentic work, and its comparisons are against GPT-6.1 Sol rather than this week's GPT-6.2. It also introduced MAI Cyber 2 Flash for security scanning and MAI Licensed Weights, which lets enterprises customise an MAI model and run it in their own Azure environment.

### [Alibaba releases Qwen4-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter model with fewer active parameters](https://www.alibabacloud.com/press-room/alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max)

**Alibaba Cloud** · 16 Nov 2026 · *Model release*

Qwen4-Max is the first model of the Qwen4 generation, a mixture-of-experts with 2.4 trillion total parameters and 71 billion active per token, a quarter fewer active than Qwen3.8-Max at the same scale, plus native vision and a 1M-token context. On its proprietary Work360 benchmark Alibaba says it beats its predecessor while using 35% fewer tokens and cutting average cost per task by about 60%, and it reports sixth place on Code Arena. The model is offered through QwenCloud and Alibaba's own apps, and the announcement promises open weights only for smaller Qwen4 models, with no dates given.

### [OpenAI lets four outside safety groups work inside its labs, with limits](https://openai.com/index/our-first-phase-of-embedded-assessments/)

**OpenAI** · 16 Nov 2026 · *Safety*

Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire will work on site with access, as each assessment requires, to checkpoints, source code, training and evaluation data and monitoring systems. Apollo will test whether models could covertly subvert their developers, METR will examine misalignment in OpenAI's largest reinforcement-learning run, a narrower scope than METR had proposed, and Transluce will review post-training alignment evaluations ahead of a longer assessment in early 2027. Assessors may publish independently, but OpenAI keeps the right to redact confidential material and request disclosure delays; Apollo and Goodfire are paid, METR and Transluce are not. OpenAI frames the pilot as part of its commitments under the White House Accord on Super Intelligence.

### [Genie 4 reaches Google AI Ultra subscribers through Project Genie](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/)

**Google** · 18 Nov 2026 · *World models*

This follows the trusted-tester announcement covered on 31 October: Genie 4 is now rolling out to eligible Ultra subscribers inside a rebuilt Project Genie, with a waitlist for Pro members. Users can type new prompts mid-session to change weather, add characters or redirect events, and the model generates matching audio in real time. Genie 3 remains available with sessions extended from 60 seconds to three minutes, alongside a conversational world-building agent and a community gallery. Google still lists camera latency, complex physics, audio precision and long-term scene permanence as open problems, and all output carries SynthID watermarks.
