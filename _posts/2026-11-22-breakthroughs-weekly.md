### [Riemann hypothesis proof found with Claude is certified by the Lean community](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf)

**Levent Alpöge** · 20 Nov 2026 · *Number theory*

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge posted a four-page note saying that, working with Claude, he has proved the Riemann hypothesis and a generalized form of it, releasing a Lean library of roughly 17 million lines instead of a written proof because Claude's attempts at prose ran past 250,000 pages. A day later the Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative, issued a joint statement certifying that the library proves exactly Mathlib's statement of the classical hypothesis, after independent rebuilds, two separate statement-comparison tools and several unrelated proof checkers all agreed. Nobody yet understands the argument, number theorists including Maksym Radziwiłł have criticised a proof that offers no explanation, and there has been no peer review. Separately, Ravi Vakil posted an entirely human 940-page draft proof of the generalized hypothesis by Stanford postdoc Alessandro Masullo, which Brian Conrad has been checking and which still awaits wider scrutiny.
